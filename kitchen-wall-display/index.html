Kitchen Wall Display

Clock, weather, calendar and notes for an old tablet on your wall.

Android 6.0+ · coming soon to Google Play

Give an old tablet or phone a new job: a calm, always-on display for your kitchen, hallway or office.

At a glance

  • Big analog clock, three faces
  • Weather for your town, every 30 minutes
  • Month calendar with birthdays and notes; today highlighted
  • Notes and shopping list with tick boxes

Made for old devices

  • Android 6.0 and newer
  • Well under 1 MB, no background services
  • Landscape and portrait, tablets and phones
  • Large text, three sizes, eight colours

Built for the wall

  • Keeps the screen on, hides the system bars
  • Night dimming on a schedule
  • Can open by itself after a power cut
  • English and Greek, 12/24h, °C/°F

Private by design

  • No account, no ads, no tracking
  • Only one permission: internet, for the weather
  • Your notes never leave the device
  • Back up to a file whenever you like

Privacy

The app collects nothing about you. Read the full privacy policy (English and Greek).
